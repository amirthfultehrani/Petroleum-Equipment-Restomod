§04 — Our History

The Benford Legacy

Established in 1991, Benford Fueling Systems, Inc started out as a service and installation company in Tuscaloosa, AL. The business quickly grew to be a full-line distributorship.

"I have to be able to lay my head on the pillow each night knowing that the work we do will stand the test of time." — Byron Benford, Founder

In March of 2000, we became the first PEI member company to offer an online catalog for petroleum handling equipment. By 2003, business was booming, and ARK Petroleum Equipment, Inc was formed—named by combining the names of Byron's three children: Amanda, Robert, and Katherine.
